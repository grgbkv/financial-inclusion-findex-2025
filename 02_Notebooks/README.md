# 02_Notebooks — the analysis

**[`financial-inclusion-findex-2025.ipynb`](financial-inclusion-findex-2025.ipynb) is the
end-to-end analysis** — the raw country file at one end, every headline series at the other, with
the validation against World Bank published aggregates woven through it rather than bolted on as
an appendix.

```bash
pip install -r ../requirements.txt
jupyter notebook financial-inclusion-findex-2025.ipynb
```

## What the notebook does, in order

1. **Split** country rows from the published aggregate rows, and merge the 2022 fieldwork into
   the 2021 wave.
2. **Build the balanced panel** — the 117 economies present in all five waves, 96.5% of the
   surveyed adult population — so that a trend reflects behaviour rather than a changing set of
   countries.
3. **Aggregate population-weighted**, matching World Bank methodology.
4. **Validate every series** against the World Bank's own aggregate, plotted as hollow markers on
   the same axes. The panel deviates from the official world account series by at most **1.5 pp**.
5. **Handle the gaps deliberately** rather than silently — mobile money is only surveyed where such
   services operate, and digital-payment coverage collapses for high-income economies in 2024.
6. **Section 7: the errata.** Rebuilding the original thesis analysis with this pipeline surfaced
   five substantive errors, two of which flipped the sign of a headline trend once country
   composition was held fixed. They are documented rather than quietly corrected.

## A note on what is not here

`FindexNotebook.ipynb` and the `figure_4.*.png` files are the original bachelor's-thesis
versions. They are gitignored: they are superseded by the notebook above and by
`THESIS_ERRATA_private.md`, and they are kept locally only as the record of what the corrections
were corrections *to*.

Figures for the repository and the README are **not** produced here — they are generated by
[`../03_Outputs/build_figures.py`](../03_Outputs/build_figures.py) so that one visual system
covers every chart. See [03_Outputs](../03_Outputs/).
